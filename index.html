Artificial Intelligence for Solid Mechanics

AI4Solid develops open tools and research practices for using AI in solid mechanics, with an emphasis on explicit assumptions, inspectable derivations, and reproducibility.

Research objectives

State assumptions explicitly

Record constitutive laws, boundary conditions, and admissibility requirements where readers can inspect them.

Verify derivations

Connect results in mechanics to precise statements and machine-checked proofs where possible.

Make AI-assisted work reproducible

Publish code, proof artifacts, and build instructions so others can examine and extend the work.
